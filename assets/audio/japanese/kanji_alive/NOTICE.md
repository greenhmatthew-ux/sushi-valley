They come from the
[Kanji alive language-data and media repository](https://github.com/kanjialive/kanji-data-media)
at revision `2d2a4931eec6e0cb532d5102766273c2323f96db` and its official
[Ogg audio archive](https://media.kanjialive.com/examples_audio/audio-ogg.zip).

The selected recordings were copied byte-for-byte from that archive. Sushi
Valley did not transcode, synthesize, or otherwise modify them.

No audio or other media from the imported Anki decks is used here. Those deck
files remain data-only sources because their bundled-media redistribution
rights have not been established.
